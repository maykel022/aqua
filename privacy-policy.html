Privacy Policy

Application: Bubble Pop Arcade (com.aqua.bubblepop)
Effective Date: October 20, 2026
Developer: Aqua Studios

Summary: Bubble Pop Arcade is designed as a privacy-friendly casual arcade puzzle game. We do not collect names, emails, contacts, phone numbers, or precise GPS locations. We utilize Google AdMob to deliver opt-in rewarded video boosts and standard advertising in accordance with Google Play policies.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Bubble Pop Arcade does not require account registration or login. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number.
  • Sensitive information (biometric data, contacts list, media library, microphone or camera access).
  • Precise geolocation data.

2. Information Automatically Processed

To provide offline progression, cloud-independent play, and optional rewarded boosts, certain information is processed locally on your device or via Google services:

A. Local Device Storage

Your game levels, unlocked biomes, quest milestones, stars, coins, and booster inventory are stored directly on your device via standard local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your device through your operating system.

B. Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

We integrate Google AdMob (Google Play Services) to provide players with optional rewarded videos (such as earning free bonus boosters or continuing extra moves) and non-intrusive ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device identifiers and diagnostics, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, device manufacturer and model.
  • Performance and Usage Metrics: Ad interaction events (views, clicks), app responsiveness, crash reports, and diagnostic logs.
  • IP Address: Coarse city/regional IP-level location to serve region-appropriate ads and prevent fraudulent impressions.

For more details on how Google processes data, please visit Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

3. In-App Purchases and Virtual Items

Virtual items, coin bundles, and boosters are acquired using in-game earned currency or via Google Play In-App Billing. All financial transactions and payment card details are handled directly and securely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

Bubble Pop Arcade is suitable for all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Google AdMob requests are flagged to respect appropriate age ratings and exclude interest-based behavioral tracking for child audiences.

5. User Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset Advertising ID.
  • Data Deletion: Because game progress is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage in your device settings will permanently erase all local game data.

6. Security

We implement standard security practices and rely on Google Play's hardened application sandboxing and encrypted communication (HTTPS/TLS) for all external network requests.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
