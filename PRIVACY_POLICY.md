# Privacy Policy for Bubble Pop Arcade

**Application:** Bubble Pop Arcade (`com.aqua.bubblepop`)  
**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026  
**Developer:** Aqua Studios  
**Contact:** support@aqua-bubblepop.com  

---

## 1. Overview
Bubble Pop Arcade is a casual puzzle game created by Aqua Studios. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data handling, advertising, and compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
Bubble Pop Arcade does not require registration, login accounts, or social network linking. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit:
- Personal Identifiable Information (legal names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses).
- Sensitive device permissions (microphone, camera, contacts, photo library, calendar).
- Precise GPS coordinates or location tracking.

## 3. Information Automatically Processed
### A. Local Storage Progression
Game save data—including levels cleared, high scores, stars, unlocked biomes, quests, coins, and booster inventory—is stored locally in the application storage sandbox on your device.

### B. Advertising via Google AdMob
We utilize Google AdMob (part of Google Play Services) to offer optional rewarded video bonuses (e.g. bonus booster items or extra moves) and banner displays. Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personal diagnostic and advertising data, including:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, hardware model, OS version.
- **Diagnostics & Performance:** Ad display events, crash logs, frame rate statistics.
- **Approximate Location:** Coarse region/country derived from IP address for localized ad serving.

For more information, see [Google Advertising Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 4. In-App Purchases
All financial transactions for optional virtual goods and coin packs are handled securely through Google Play In-App Billing. Aqua Studios does not collect or store credit card numbers or financial information.

## 5. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)
Bubble Pop Arcade is designed for players of all ages and complies with COPPA and Google Play Families Policies. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). AdMob advertising requests are configured in compliance with family-safe guidelines.

## 6. User Rights & Data Control (GDPR / CCPA)
- **Advertising Choices:** You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android *Settings > Google > Ads*.
- **Data Erasure:** Uninstalling the game or clearing application storage will immediately erase all saved local progress.

For any privacy-related inquiries, please reach out to:
